About FaceShield AI

FaceShield AI is an Artificial Intelligence-based Face Privacy Protection System designed to safeguard facial identity while preserving the natural appearance of images.

The system combines MediaPipe Face Landmarker, OpenCV and InsightFace to detect facial landmarks, selectively protect identity-sensitive regions, and evaluate privacy using facial similarity analysis.

🛡 Smart Privacy

AI intelligently protects facial identity without hiding the entire face.

⚡ Fast Processing

Image protection is completed within seconds using optimized AI models.

📊 Privacy Analysis

Calculates similarity score and privacy percentage automatically.

🔒 Secure Protection

Protects sensitive facial landmarks while maintaining image quality.
